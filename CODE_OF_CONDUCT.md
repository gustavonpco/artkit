# Code of conduct

artkit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban participants who violate these rules.
